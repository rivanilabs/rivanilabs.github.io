Set up a free AI model

How to turn on "detailed scan" for free.

HealthFolio works out of the box with no AI key at all — the free, on-device scan reads the headline details of a document offline. Turning on an AI model unlocks detailed scan: full line items, findings, and richer extraction. You can do this for free with Google Gemini's free tier.

Free-tier limits and model names change over time — see each provider's rate-limits page (linked below) for current details. Anthropic and OpenAI do not offer a free API tier; they require adding a payment method.

Recommended: Google Gemini (free)

  1. Go to Google AI Studio → API keys and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key. Copy the key (it starts with AIza…).
  3. In HealthFolio, open Settings and turn on Detailed scanning.
  4. Choose Gemini (Google) as the provider and paste your key.
  5. Pick a free-tier model (a Gemini Flash Lite model is the cheapest and works well). Keep Redact personal info before upload switched on.
  6. Scan a document and choose detailed scan. That's it.

Free-tier requests are capped per day and reset daily. If you hit the limit, wait for the reset or switch back to the free on-device scan. Current limits: Gemini API rate limits.

Is my data safe?

Your API key is stored in your device's Keychain and never sent to us. When you run a detailed scan, the document goes directly from your device to the provider you chose, using your own key, and personal identifiers are redacted first (by default). Your use of the provider is governed by that provider's terms. See our Privacy Policy.
