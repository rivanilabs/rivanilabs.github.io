Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

HealthFolio is developed by Rivani Labs. In short: your records stay on your device, and we do not run any server that receives them.

We have no account and no server

HealthFolio does not require you to create an account. There is no HealthFolio login, and we do not operate a backend service that stores your data. We cannot see your records.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter or scan — family members, pharmacy bills, prescriptions, lab and diagnostic reports, hospital admissions, and the document images/PDFs you scan — is stored locally on your device. These files are included in your normal iCloud Backup if you have it enabled (that backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us).

Face ID

If you enable it, Face ID is used to unlock the app on your device. This happens entirely on your device; we never receive biometric data.

Optional "detailed scan" — the only time data leaves your device

The app can optionally use a third-party AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google) to read a scanned document in more detail. This feature is off by default and only runs when you turn it on and provide your own API key for that provider.

  • A copy of the scanned document is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you selected, using your own API key. We are not an intermediary and do not receive it.
  • By default, the app redacts personal identifiers (such as name, date of birth, ID numbers, phone, email, and address) from the document before it is sent.
  • Your use of that provider is governed by that provider's privacy policy and terms: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google.

Your API keys are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and are never sent to us.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.
  • We do not use tracking or advertising, and we do not share data with data brokers.
  • We do not sell your information.

Purchases

Paid features (Claim-Ready) are handled by Apple's In-App Purchase system. We receive purchase status from Apple to unlock features on your device; we do not receive your payment details.

Your control and deletion

Because your data is on your device, you are in control of it. You can delete individual records in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data from your device (subject to any iCloud Backup you manage through Apple).

Children

HealthFolio is a family record-keeping tool intended for use by adults managing their household's records. It is not directed at children.

Legal

We handle personal data in line with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Apple's App Store requirements. Because records are stored on your device and not on our servers, most processing happens under your own control.

Not medical advice

HealthFolio is a record-keeping tool, not a medical device. AI-generated summaries may be inaccurate and are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified doctor.
