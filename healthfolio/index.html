HealthFolio

Your family's medical records — private, on your device.

What it does

HealthFolio scans and organizes your family's pharmacy bills, prescriptions, lab reports and hospital discharge summaries — then builds an insurer-ready claim package in a couple of taps.

  • Scan & auto-organize medical documents by family member
  • See a visual "patient journey" timeline of visits, admissions and receipts
  • Generate a categorized, insurer-ready claim PDF

Private by design

Records are stored on your device — there is no HealthFolio account and no HealthFolio server. An optional AI "detailed scan" uses a provider you choose, with your own API key, and redacts personal identifiers before anything is sent. It's off unless you turn it on.
