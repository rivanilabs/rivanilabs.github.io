Getting started

How do I add a family member?

Open the app and tap to add a new person. Each family member gets their own private set of records.

How do I scan a document?

Use the camera to scan a bill, prescription, lab report, or discharge summary, or import a PDF from Files. HealthFolio reads the key details on-device and files it under the right person.

How does HealthFolio link my records?

A pharmacy bill or prescription is automatically linked to the visit or hospital stay that most likely caused it (by date). You can review and fix any link from the record's detail screen, or from the "Patient journey" view when building a claim.

AI & detailed scan

Do I need to pay for AI?

No. The basic scan runs on your device for free. For richer "detailed scan" you can use Google Gemini's free tier with your own key — see Set up a free AI model.

Where does my scanned document go?

Nowhere, unless you turn on detailed scan. Then a redacted copy is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you chose, using your own key. See our Privacy Policy.

Purchases

What does Claim-Ready unlock?

Unlimited family members, unlimited detailed scans, AI health summaries, and one-click insurer-ready claim PDFs. It's available as a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or App Store → your profile → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the current period ends.

I bought Claim-Ready on another device.

Use Restore Purchases on the paywall screen to restore your entitlement.

Privacy & data

Where is my data stored?

On your device. There's no HealthFolio account and no HealthFolio server. Your records are included in your normal iCloud Backup if you have it enabled.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records inside the app, or delete the app to remove its local data from your device.
