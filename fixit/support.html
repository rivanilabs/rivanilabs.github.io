How to play

Customers bring broken products to your workshop counter. Tap the tools shown under each item — in the right order — to fix it before the customer's patience bar runs out. Earn money and star reviews for good work, restock your tools in the Shop when you run low, and choose your challenge from Easy all the way up to Impossible.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection? No — Fix It plays fully offline.

Is my data safe? Yes. The game collects no personal data; everything is stored on your device only.

The game is too hard / too easy. Tap the gear on the title screen or the difficulty cards to switch between Easy, Normal, Hard, and Impossible.
