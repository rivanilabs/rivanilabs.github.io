Privacy Policy — Fix It

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Fix It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The game works fully offline and requires no account or sign-in.

All game data — your progress, high score, star rating, and settings — is stored only on your device and never leaves it. Fix It contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Because we collect no data, we have nothing to sell, share, or disclose to anyone. Fix It is suitable for players of all ages.
